Professional Summary

I am an Assistant Professor of Computer Science in the Department of Mathematics and Computer Science at Louisiana State University of Alexandria (LSUA). I have extensive experience teaching a wide range of computer science (CS) courses. My research in Medical Image Machine Learning, Neural Network, Deep Learning, Computer Vision, Distributed Computing, and Parallel Computing allow me to continually update and enhance my teaching, moving beyond legacy courses to provide students with the latest developments and practical applications in the field. My objective is to leverage my combined teaching and research experience to deliver high-quality instruction, mentor students, contribute to curriculum development, and foster an engaging learning environment that reflects current advancements in computer science.

Biograohy

Ahmed Sharafeldeen received the Ph.D. degree in Computer Science and Engineering from the University of Louisville, USA, in 2026, and the B.Sc. and M.Sc. degrees in Computer Science from Mansoura University, Egypt, in 2013 and 2018, respectively. He is currently an Assistant Professor of Computer Science in the Department of Mathematics and Computer Science at Louisiana State University at Alexandria (LSUA), USA. Prior to joining LSUA, he served as a Graduate Teaching Assistant and researcher at the University of Louisville and as an Assistant Lecturer in the Department of Computer Science, Faculty of Computers and Information, Mansoura University, Egypt. He has authored or co-authored multiple peer-reviewed journal articles, conference papers, book chapters, and other scholarly publications, and has served as a reviewer for several peer-reviewed journals. His research interests include artificial intelligence, machine learning, deep learning, neural networks, computer vision, medical image analysis, image processing, distributed and parallel computing, and big data. His research focuses particularly on the application of artificial intelligence, machine learning, and deep learning techniques to medical imaging and computer-aided diagnosis.

Software

Sarcopenia Assessment Software (2024, Radiology Department, University of Louisville)
Developed a software application to evaluate sarcopenia using CT images. Sarcopenia, a condition characterized by the gradual loss of muscle mass, strength, and function, primarily affects older adults, leading to weakness, reduced endurance, and increased risk of falls, fractures, and disabilities. The software automatically delineates the muscle region around the L3 vertebral level and calculates the Skeletal Muscle Index (SMI) based on the segmented area and the patient’s height, providing clinicians with a quantitative and reliable tool to assess muscle loss.
